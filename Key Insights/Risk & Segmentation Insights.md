# Risk & Profitability Insights

## 1. How was risk analysis performed?

Risk analysis was performed using:

* Account Balance
* Transaction Activity
* Customer Behavior

## 2. How were customers categorized based on risk?

Customers were categorized into:

* Low Risk
* Medium Risk
* High Risk

## 3. What does the scatter plot represent?

The scatter plot analyzes the relationship between:

* Transaction Amount
* Account Balance

This helps identify:

* High-value customers
* Risky customers
* Unusual transaction behavior

## 4. What business value does this analysis provide?

This analysis helps banking organizations:

* identify profitable customers,
* monitor risky customer behavior,
* improve customer targeting,
* and support better business decision-making.

## 5. What is the importance of RFM analysis?

RFM analysis helps understand:

* Recency
* Frequency
* Monetary contribution

This supports customer retention strategies and customer relationship management.
