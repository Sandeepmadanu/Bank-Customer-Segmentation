# Customer Segmentation Insights

## 1. How many loyal customers are there?

There are approximately 27K loyal customers.

## 2. How much revenue is generated from new customers?

New customers generated approximately ₹9.51M revenue.

## 3. How many lost customers are there?

There are approximately 63K lost customers.

## 4. Which customer segment has the highest revenue contribution?

Loyal customers contribute the highest overall revenue.

## 5. How are customers distributed across segments?

* Lost Customers → 39.43%
* New Customers → 34.24%
* Loyal Customers → 16.63%
* Regular Customers → 9.69%

## 6. How does total revenue differ across customer segments?

* Loyal Customers → ₹10.20M
* New Customers → ₹9.51M
* Lost Customers → ₹7.64M
* Regular Customers → ₹3.91M

## 7. Which segment has the highest transaction frequency?

Lost customers show the highest transaction frequency with approximately 62,707 transactions.

## 8. What business insight was observed from segmentation analysis?

Loyal customers form the most valuable customer segment with strong revenue contribution and higher average account balance.
