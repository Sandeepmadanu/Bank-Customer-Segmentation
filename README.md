# 🏦 Bank Customer Segmentation

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project focuses on analyzing banking customer behavior, transaction activity, customer segmentation, profitability, and risk indicators using interactive dashboards and business intelligence techniques.

The objective of this project is to generate meaningful business insights from banking transaction data and improve understanding of customer demographics, transaction trends, customer contribution, and customer behavior patterns.

✔ Customer Demographics Analysis
✔ Transaction Behavior Analysis
✔ Customer Segmentation Analysis

✔ Revenue Contribution Analysis
✔ Business Insights & Dashboard Storytelling

---

# 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Basic data cleaning and preparation were performed in Power BI before creating dashboards.

The major preparation steps included:

* Formatting transaction date and time columns
* Creating Age column from Date of Birth
* Creating Age Group categories
* Extracting Hour from transaction time
* Creating Customer Segments such as Loyal, New, Lost, and Regular
* Creating Risk Level categories
* Generating DAX measures for KPI calculations
* Organizing and filtering visuals for better dashboard analysis

These steps helped prepare the dataset for interactive business intelligence reporting.

---

# 📈 Key Insights Observed

* Loyal customers contributed the highest revenue among all customer segments.
* Male customers formed the majority customer base in the dataset.
* Customers aged between 35–44 represented the largest age group.
* Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore showed the highest customer concentration.
* Transaction activity fluctuated across different dates and time periods.
* Risk analysis helped identify high-value and potentially risky customer groups.
* Interactive dashboards enabled dynamic filtering and business insight generation.
